# Splines and spline model edges

Author a spline with `create_sketch`/`update_sketch` as a chain of `cubic-bezier` entities: each segment's `endPointId` is the next one's `startPointId`, and a closed spline ends at its first start point. Add one `tangent-join` (`incomingCurveId`, `outgoingCurveId`) per interior join and, when closed, for the closing join. Each solve checks G1: a cusp seed, a reversed join or a handle under 1% of its segment chord returns `failed` with that join ID. Seed handles on the intended tangent at about a third of each chord, and do not restate the tangency with other constraints. G2 joins are unavailable.

To reference a planar B-spline or Bezier model edge, add one `model-spline` with `N` (at most 64) fresh `projectedCurveIds` and `3N + 1` fresh `projectedPointIds` in chained order. Build `reference` from the inspected `model_body_topology` or `draft_body_topology` candidate (`featureId`, `kind: "edge"`, its `semanticRole` or single lineage token, and `signature`; never the candidate ID). Set `endpointAnchors` to sketch-local `[x, y]` points near the intended start and end, such as its end vertices projected onto the sketch; each solve pairs the edge ends with them by smaller displacement, and an equally plausible pairing fails closed. The fit is not exposed: start with the smallest plausible `N` and raise it if the solution reports that more segments are needed. Edges must lie in a plane parallel to the sketch; tilted, spatial, periodic, uncertifiable (1e-4 mm), other non-analytical and missing or ambiguous edges fail closed. Constrain authored points only to the first and last `projectedPointIds`; interior points and projected curves are read-only and rejected as targets. Repair keeps `N` and replaces `endpointAnchors`, so the new edge must fit in `N` segments.
