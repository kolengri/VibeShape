# Splines and spline model edges

Author a spline with `create_sketch`/`update_sketch` as a chain of `cubic-bezier` entities: each segment's `endPointId` is the next one's `startPointId`, and a closed spline ends at its first start point. Add one `tangent-join` (`incomingCurveId`, `outgoingCurveId`) per interior join and, when closed, for the closing join. Each solve checks G1: a cusp seed, a reversed join or a handle under 1% of its segment chord returns `failed` with that join ID. Seed handles on the intended tangent at about a third of each chord, and do not restate the tangency with other constraints. G2 joins are unavailable.

To reference a planar B-spline or Bezier model edge, add one `model-spline` with `N` (at most 64) fresh `projectedCurveIds` and `3N + 1` fresh `projectedPointIds` in chained order. Build `reference` from the inspected `model_body_topology` or `draft_body_topology` candidate (`featureId`, `kind: "edge"`, its `semanticRole` or single lineage token, and `signature`; never the candidate ID). Omit `endpointAnchors` unless you know the sketch-local `[x, y]` positions of the intended start and end: `create_sketch` and `update_sketch` fill omitted anchors from the edge's current projected ends, as Use does, and keep supplied ones. Each solve pairs the edge ends with the anchors by smaller displacement, and an equally plausible pairing fails closed. The fit is not exposed: start with the smallest plausible `N` and raise it if the solution reports that more segments are needed. Edges must lie in a plane parallel to the sketch; tilted, spatial, periodic, uncertifiable (1e-4 mm), other non-analytical and missing or ambiguous edges fail closed. Constrain authored points only to the first and last `projectedPointIds`; interior points and projected curves are read-only and rejected as targets. To repair, keep `N` and the projected IDs, replace `reference`, and omit `endpointAnchors` so they are re-anchored on the new edge; it must fit in `N` segments.
