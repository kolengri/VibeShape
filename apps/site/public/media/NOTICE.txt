Hero visual provenance

The opening boat uses the original 3DBenchy single-part mesh by Creative Tools.
Source: https://github.com/CreativeTools/3DBenchy/blob/master/Single-part/3DBenchy.stl
License: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://www.3dbenchy.com/license/

The mechanical housing, contour transformation, and rendering are original presentation artwork for VibeShape.
This is an illustration, not a recording of the VibeShape editor.

hero-model.jpg is the exploded-housing frame extracted from the presentation video at four seconds.
hero-float.mp4 is an eight-second eased forward/reverse loop derived from frames 109–131 of
hero-scroll.mp4, with optical-flow interpolation to 60 fps. It retains the original artwork's provenance.
hero-intro-return.mp4 contains the original six-second introduction, followed by its final two
source seconds in reverse with eased timing over three seconds and interpolation to 60 fps.
